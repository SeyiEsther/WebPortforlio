Velvet Nocturne

View Project ↗

Role

Art Director & Web Designer

Year

2024

Services

Brand Identity · Web Design · Interaction Design · SVG Animation

Context

An independent fragrance house needed a brand world that matched the emotional depth of their scents — not just a storefront, but an experience. Visitors were converting at under 1.2%, and the brand had no coherent digital identity to anchor its premium positioning.

Decision

Led with atmosphere over information. Built a scroll-driven dark experience where motion, sound-responsive elements, and sparse typography let users feel each fragrance before they read it. Navigation was made invisible until needed, keeping immersion unbroken.

Outcome

Conversion rate increased to 3.8% within 60 days of launch. Average session duration doubled. The brand received editorial coverage in Vogue Beauty and Wallpaper*, attributing the new site as a key driver of their renewed prestige positioning.

Terrain

View Project ↗

Role

Lead Designer & Creative Director

Year

2024

Services

Motion Design · Editorial Layout · Web Typography · Scroll Engineering

Context

A leading landscape architecture studio needed to communicate complex, spatial thinking to clients, press, and collaborators who weren't spatial specialists. Their existing site was a static image gallery with no narrative structure — a portfolio with no story.

Decision

Built a scroll-driven editorial experience where topographic map logic became the navigation — users move through the site the way you move through terrain. Typography does the heavy lifting: oversized type sets scale, while precise micro-copy carries technical depth.

Outcome

Inquiry quality tripled in the first quarter post-launch, with clients arriving pre-educated on the studio's methodology. Featured in Dezeen and Architectural Digest. Studio director described it as "the first time our website felt like our work."

03 /

Luminary

View Project ↗

Role

Web Designer & Brand Identity

Year

2023

Services

Web Design · Brand Identity · Illustration · Digital Art Direction

Context

A theatre and arts collective needed an event microsite that could attract both ticket-buying audiences and institutional funding bodies simultaneously — two groups with radically different triggers, both needing to be convinced of artistic legitimacy and commercial viability.

Decision

Used theatrical metaphor as the structural logic: the site performs before it informs. Stage lighting, dramatic reveals, and a deliberate tension between darkness and sudden illumination meant visitors experienced the brand before they read a single word of copy.

Outcome

Inaugural season sold out within 72 hours of launch. £40,000 secured in new sponsorships from three arts foundations. The collective's press officer noted multiple journalists had screenshotted the website homepage unprompted in reviews — treating it as part of the artistic work itself.

About

I'm Shae — a web artist who treats the browser as a canvas and every project as a story waiting to be told. Based in London, I've spent the past several years at the intersection of art direction, editorial design, and interaction engineering.

My work starts with feeling. Before I sketch a wireframe, I ask: what should this make you feel? Then I build backwards from that emotional brief into a system rigorous enough to ship and alive enough to matter.

I'm currently seeking to collaborate with studios that believe the web is still a creative frontier — not a solved problem.
